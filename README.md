# What makes for a great morning?

A short recitation for beginning the day. Said out loud, one phrase at a
time. It isn't a prayer and it isn't a productivity trick — it's a list of
what you want to be true about how you meet a day.

**Read it, print it, or download it:** <https://mornings.maketimeflow.com>

## The text

- [`recitations/individual.md`](recitations/individual.md) — the recitation,
  and the text the app reads.
- [`recitations/template.md`](recitations/template.md) — the same shape with
  the words taken out, to write your own.
- [`downloads/`](downloads/) — the same text as PDF, Word and plain text.

## Make it yours

The words are yours to change. Fork this repository and rewrite
`recitations/individual.md`, or download the Word version and edit it like
any other document, or paste your own text straight into the app — it keeps
a library of your own recitations, privately, on your phone.

I'm not collecting other people's versions here, so there's no need to send
one back. Write the one that's true for you and say that instead.

## Say it with the app

Great Mornings is an iPhone app: one invitation at the hour you choose, the
words one phrase at a time, with my reading or in silence. No account, no
streaks, nothing collected. It's in open beta —
[join on TestFlight](https://testflight.apple.com/join/s5JQ764d).
