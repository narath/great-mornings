# What makes for a great morning?

A short recitation for beginning the day. Said out loud, one phrase at a
time. It isn't a prayer and it isn't a productivity trick — it's a list of
what you want to be true about how you meet a day. 

If part or all of it does not resonate with you, then make your own (see below). 

**Read it, print it, or download it:** <https://mornings.maketimeflow.com>

This repository is the words themselves, so that forking it gets you
something to rewrite rather than a website to maintain.

## What's here

- [`recitations/individual.md`](recitations/individual.md) — the recitation.
  The same text the iPhone app reads.
- [`recitations/template.md`](recitations/template.md) — the same shape with
  the words taken out, to write your own.
- [`downloads/`](downloads/) — the recitation as a one-page
  [PDF](downloads/great-mornings-recitation.pdf), as an editable
  [Word document](downloads/great-mornings-recitation.docx), and as
  [plain text](downloads/great-mornings-recitation.txt).

## Make it yours

The words are mine, and they're yours to change. Fork this repository and
rewrite `recitations/individual.md`; or download the Word version and edit it
like any other document; or paste your own straight into the app, which keeps
a library of your own recitations, privately, on your phone.

If starting from a blank page is easier than starting from mine, the template
has the shape and none of the words.

Write the one that's true for you, and say that instead.

## If you want help getting started

There is an iPhone app. One invitation, at the hour you choose. Say the words
with me, or I read it for you, or you read it yourself. No account, no streaks.
It's built to be learned and put down. It's in open beta —
[join on TestFlight](https://testflight.apple.com/join/s5JQ764d).
